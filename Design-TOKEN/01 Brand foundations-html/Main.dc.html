RAKA is named after Rakaposhi, the Karakoram peak in Gilgit-Baltistan. The store looks like that summit in daylight: snow-bright ground, warm copper accents and a little alpenglow for play. Clean enough to read every ingredient, styled enough to feel like a fashion editorial.

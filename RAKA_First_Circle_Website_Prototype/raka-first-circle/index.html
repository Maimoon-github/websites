04 — THE DETAILS

Good to
know.

What is RAKA First Circle? +

It's RAKA's free pre-launch community. Members who register with email permission will receive our welcome guide and occasional launch updates.

When are the products launching? +

We're preparing for a launch in the coming months. The confirmed launch date will be shared through RAKA's official website and opted-in updates.

Will I see the products before launch? +

Not yet. We're intentionally keeping product and packaging visuals private until the official reveal. In the meantime, we'll share the thinking and education behind the brand.

Is registration free? +

Yes. There's no registration cost and no purchase requirement. Membership doesn't guarantee a discount, reserved inventory, or priority checkout.

Will you contact me on WhatsApp? +

Only if you separately select WhatsApp updates and that service is activated. The email signup and optional SMS or WhatsApp preferences are kept distinct.

Can I unsubscribe or ask for my data to be removed? +

Yes. Live email messages must include a working unsubscribe mechanism, and the published Privacy Notice will explain how to access, update, or request deletion of your details.

DRAFT — REVIEW BEFORE LAUNCH

Privacy Notice.

This notice describes the planned RAKA First Circle registration experience. This version is for prototype review and must be finalized with RAKA's real contact details, processors, retention rules, and legal review before publication.

What we collect

Your optional first name, email address, Pakistani mobile number, selected communication preferences, referral code, registration time, and basic campaign attribution information.

Why we collect it

To administer First Circle registrations, deliver a welcome guide, and send the specific launch communications you request. Referral information, where provided, helps attribute invited signups.

Communication choices

Joining requires consent to First Circle emails. WhatsApp and SMS are independent optional choices. Each channel must support appropriate opt-out and preference updates. Marketing must not be sent to an unconsented channel.

Who handles the information

In a real deployment, RAKA would use approved hosting, database, and messaging providers acting under appropriate data-processing terms. Their identities and any cross-border transfers should be described in the final notice. This prototype sends no registration information to a server.

Retention and your choices

Define and publish a practical retention period before launching. Members should be able to withdraw consent, request access or correction, and request deletion subject to applicable requirements.

Contact and effective date

Add a monitored privacy contact address, legal entity details, effective date, and any Pakistan-specific disclosures after confirmation. Do not publish this draft as final legal wording.
